# 05 — The budget record states operational facts about the company account

**Blocked by:** None — can start immediately.

**Status:** superseded

## What happens

The budget-guard record states that the account this tool is built for has never reached its daily
token budget, and the testing record states that the live suite runs against the real company
account with no sandbox tenant. These are observations about a named company's Pipedrive usage.

No figures, seat counts or tier names appear, so the disclosure is small. It is still a statement
about someone else's account that becomes public with the repository.

## What I expected

A public document either omits the observation or states it without tying it to a specific account.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Read the budget-guard record's sections on how the daily ceiling behaves in practice.
2. Read the testing record's paragraph on where the live suite points.

## Additional context

The design of the daily budget ceiling is settled and is not in question here — only whether the
prose needs to attribute the observation to a real account. Rewriting the sentence to speak about
"the account a run points at" preserves the reasoning and drops the attribution.
